How we checked it

Season by season

Corn: 14 days around silking, estimated from capped growing degree days after green-up. Soybean: from 14 days before to 21 days after the greenness peak. Flag: root-zone wetness below the 20th percentile on half the window, or daily maximum above the 80th percentile on 5 of 14 days (provisional). The three fields sit in two neighbouring regional weather cells, so the same years recur; which crop is hit depends on the rotation’s phase. Confidence is capped at moderate, or moderate–high where the field-scale ECOSTRESS stress index (actual over potential evapotranspiration, 70 m, within 10 days of the window, against this field’s own summer median) confirms a flag. ECOSTRESS overpass times vary, so read single seasons with care.

Regional context from NASA POWER (about 0.5°), 15 June–15 August each year. Root-zone wetness is modelled (NASA GMAO MERRA-2). Grey band: middle 60% of 1991–2020. Dots: this field’s corn seasons.
